CleanLink

Report a waste spot by uploading a photo. The location is read from the photo's GPS data and turned into a city and locality. Each spot is sized, and the size decides how many local workers the cleanup needs: small 2, medium 5, large 10. A moderator checks each report, and once it is verified anyone can fund its cleanup at Rs 500 per worker. Sign up to report, donate or work.
